## Scope and mode

Single-page React cybersecurity portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience with Persuade conversion requirements for both hiring and freelance audiences.

## Audience, job, action, and proof

Hiring managers and prospective clients must understand Kunal's security-engineering identity, inspect credible case studies, and choose either the employment/resume path or the project-inquiry/services path. Flagship proof is the Nessus-to-GLPI workflow that reduced ticket-creation turnaround from approximately five days to under five minutes. Supporting proof includes security ticket-gap automation, an SSO/SCIM internal portal, BEC response, a 185+ vulnerability-remediation program, Microsoft identity and endpoint security, monitoring, network security, and governance.

## Constraints

Static React/Vite/TypeScript build deployable to GitHub Pages. No invented employers, clients, testimonials, certifications, links, adoption metrics, or confidential implementation details. Resume, headshot, email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Fiverr, and Upwork use the approved public assets and destinations. Keyboard, contrast, responsive, and reduced-motion requirements apply.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Change-Control Folio: enterprise architecture blueprints, carbon-copy work orders, routed system diagrams, and inspection stamps make every claim auditable as problem, intervention, evidence, and outcome. The memorable opening transformation is a ghosted `5 DAYS` state routed through the vulnerability workflow into a verified `<5 MIN` stamp. Direction sketch: `.impeccable/sketches/change-control-folio.webp` (approved).

Approved composition: Evidence Docket. A full-width blueprint masthead opens into an asymmetrical folded dossier: a calm professional statement at left, an oversized `<5 MIN` inspection stamp over the ghosted `5 DAYS` state at right, and a punched case index at the edge. The next fold exposes a dark Problem / Intervention / Outcome evidence ledger with separate Hiring and Client Work actions. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-c-evidence-docket.webp`.

Composition grammar: square or near-square corners; fine blueprint rules and pencil leaders; no generic card shells; flat paper overlap rather than digital elevation; condensed industrial display type against highly legible body prose; warm-paper fields alternate with full-width navy blueprint ledgers. Paper and blueprint grain are image-native textures; grids, perforations, routes, stamps, and diagrams remain semantic or authored SVG/CSS. The mobile composition becomes one reading column, moves the outcome stamp after the opening statement, turns the punched edge index into a horizontal case strip, and stacks evidence-ledger rows without changing their Problem / Intervention / Outcome order.
